Delete your Parts Hunter account

Use this page to request deletion of your Parts Hunter account and personal data — even if you no longer have the app installed. If you still have the app, you can also delete your account instantly in Settings → Delete account.

What gets deleted: your name, email address, phone number, password, and profile photo, and your account is permanently deactivated.

What we keep: completed order and payment records in anonymized form (no longer linked to you), as required for tax, accounting, and dispute-resolution obligations. See our privacy policy for details.

Request received

If that email address matches a Parts Hunter account, your deletion request has been logged and will be reviewed and processed within 30 days. You don't need to do anything else.

Questions in the meantime? Contact support@partshunter.co.uk.
