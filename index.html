Refund Policy

Effective: 1 January 2025  |  Last Updated: March 2025  |  Governed by: KAWHUDS GROUP LTD

As Partshunter is a marketplace, primary refund obligations rest with the seller. However, Partshunter will mediate all disputes and may facilitate refunds of platform-processed payments where justified under this Policy.

1. Eligible Refund Circumstances

A refund may be approved in the following circumstances only:

  • Item not delivered: The buyer paid for delivery and the part has not been received within twice the seller's stated delivery period, with no tracking update for 72 hours and the seller is unable to provide proof of dispatch.
  • Item significantly not as described: The part received is materially different from the seller's accepted quote (e.g., wrong part number, wrong condition, wrong brand where specified, wrong fitment).
  • Seller cancellation: The seller cancels an accepted order after payment has been received.
  • Duplicate payment: A verified technical error caused the buyer to be charged more than once for the same order.
  • Fraudulent seller: A seller is confirmed to have operated fraudulently on the Platform.

2. Non-Refundable Circumstances

  • Change of mind after the order has been accepted and dispatched.
  • Buyer provided incorrect vehicle details or selected an incompatible part — compatibility verification is the buyer's sole responsibility before accepting any quote.
  • Normal wear, cosmetic imperfections, or condition consistent with what was disclosed for used or reconditioned parts.
  • Delivery delays caused by third-party couriers, customs delays, force majeure, or events outside the seller's reasonable control.
  • Parts that have been installed, modified, used, or altered in any way after delivery.
  • Disputes raised more than 7 days after the confirmed delivery date.
  • Parts whose incompatibility is discovered only after a buyer has made changes to their vehicle.

3. How to Request a Refund

  1. Submit a dispute via the in-app dispute centre or email disputes@partshunter.co.uk within 48 hours of the issue arising (or within 48 hours of the expected delivery date for non-delivery claims).
  2. Include: order reference number, description of the issue, clear photographic or video evidence, and all relevant communications with the seller.
  3. Partshunter will acknowledge the dispute within 2 working days and may request additional supporting information.
  4. A decision will be communicated within 5–10 working days of receiving all required information.

4. Refund Method & Timeline

  • Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method only.
  • Timeline: 3–10 working days after approval, subject to the buyer's bank or payment provider.
  • The 20% platform commission is refundable only where the seller is found at fault or has cancelled. It is non-refundable in all other circumstances.

5. Partial Refunds

Partshunter may determine that a partial refund is appropriate — for example, where part of a multi-item order was affected, or where a minor discrepancy existed. The basis for any partial refund will be communicated clearly in writing.

6. Escalation

If you are dissatisfied with a refund decision, you may escalate in writing to management@partshunter.co.uk. All statutory rights under applicable law are preserved regardless of this internal process.

7. Fraudulent Refund Claims

Any user found to have submitted a fraudulent, fabricated, or exaggerated refund claim will have their account immediately suspended and may face legal action and/or reporting to relevant authorities. Partshunter reserves the right to pursue recovery of any amounts paid out on the basis of fraudulent claims.

Return Policy

Eligible Returns

Returns are accepted only in the following circumstances:

  • Wrong item supplied: The seller dispatched a part materially different from the accepted quote — different part number, different fitment, different brand where brand was specified.
  • Undisclosed defect: The part is non-functional or defective in a way not disclosed in the seller's description or photos at the time of quoting.
  • Damaged in transit: The part arrived visibly damaged, and the buyer can demonstrate through contemporaneous evidence (photos taken immediately upon opening) that the damage was caused during transit and was not pre-existing.

Non-Returnable Items

  • Parts that have been installed into a vehicle in any way.
  • Parts that have been modified, cut, drilled, painted, or otherwise altered.
  • Electrical components (ECUs, sensors, control modules) once connected to a vehicle's electrical system, regardless of outcome.
  • Parts where the sole complaint is an incompatibility not caused by seller misdescription.
  • Claims raised more than 48 hours after delivery confirmation.
  • Used or reconditioned parts where condition was accurately described and consistent with the item received.
  • Parts where packaging was discarded and cannot be assessed for transit damage.

Return Process

  1. Report the issue within 48 hours of receiving the item via the in-app dispute centre or at disputes@partshunter.co.uk.
  2. Provide photographic or video evidence, your order reference, and a clear description of the fault or mismatch.
  3. Partshunter will review evidence and determine whether the return qualifies within 3 working days.
  4. If approved, Partshunter will issue a Return Authorisation Number (RAN). Do not return any item without a valid RAN. Unauthorised returns will not be processed or credited.
  5. Items must be returned in the same condition as received, securely packaged, with all original components where applicable.
  6. Upon receipt and verification by the seller, a refund or exchange will be processed under the relevant policy.

Return Logistics & Costs

  • Where the return is due to seller fault (wrong item or undisclosed defect): the seller must provide a prepaid return label or arrange collection at their own expense within 3 working days of the RAN being issued.
  • Where the return is not due to seller fault: the buyer bears all return shipping costs and is responsible for ensuring safe return of the item.
  • Partshunter does not arrange return logistics. Return arrangements are made between buyer and seller under Partshunter oversight.
  • Partshunter accepts no liability for items lost or damaged during return transit.

Exchange Policy

When Exchanges Are Available

An exchange may be arranged where:

  • The return qualifies under the criteria above, AND
  • The seller confirms they have the correct replacement part in stock and available, AND
  • Both buyer and seller agree to the exchange in writing via the Platform.

Exchange Process

  • All exchange logistics and associated costs are to be agreed directly between buyer and seller, with Partshunter acting as a facilitator and mediator.
  • The replacement part must be dispatched within the same timeframe stated for the original order.
  • Where no suitable replacement is available or agreed, a full refund will be processed under the Refund Policy.
  • Exchanges are a one-time remedy per order. If a replacement item also has issues, the matter will be treated as a fresh dispute.

Partshunter's Mediation Role

Partshunter mediates return and exchange disputes but is not a party to the sale contract. We will use commercially reasonable efforts to facilitate fair outcomes. Sellers who repeatedly fail to honour legitimate return requests will face account suspension. Buyers who abuse the returns process may also face account action.

For all return and exchange queries: disputes@partshunter.co.uk
